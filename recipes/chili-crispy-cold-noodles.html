Chili Crisp Cold Noodles

Description

These chili crisp cold noodles are the perfect hot weather dish. Extraordinarily flavorful, they come together very quickly and can be used as a base for sliced meats, served over a bowl of lettuce, or just as they are.

Ingredients

  • udon noodles, or any noodle or pasta
  • 1/3 cup unsweetened smooth peanut butter
  • 2 tablespoons rice wine vinegar
  • 3 cloves garlic, crushed

Steps

  1. Boil noodles
  2. Make sauce
  3. Drain noodles, dip it in cold water and drain again
  4. Prepare the vegetables
  5. Toss everything thoroughly
  6. Serve with chili crip dolloped over the top
